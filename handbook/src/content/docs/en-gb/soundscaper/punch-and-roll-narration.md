Hear a short lead-in before recording and quickly retry a take.

## Before you start

Connect your microphone and check [recording setup](/soundscaper/recording/). Use headphones to keep the lead-in out of the microphone. Save a snapshot before replacing valuable narration.

## Steps

1. Set the intended recording position and target using your normal recording workflow.
2. Open the Record button’s **Record options** menu. Enable **Lead-in time** and open **Lead-in time settings** to set the **Lead-in duration**.
3. Close the settings and start recording. Listen through the lead-in, then speak when recording reaches the punch position.
4. If the take goes wrong, reopen **Record options** and choose **Punch again**. Stop when satisfied.

## Check the result

Listen across the entry point and the recorded phrase. The retry should use the same punch position and configured lead-in. **Undo** can return you to the preceding edit.

## Limits and recovery

Lead-in is optional and its setting persists. **Punch again** needs an eligible previous recording attempt; it is disabled before one exists. Stop recording before changing the duration. Check the actual recording target before retrying so you replace the intended passage.
