Assess a damaged project and save recovered work as a separate copy.

## Before you start

Make a copy of the damaged Audacity project first. Keep any available sidecar files together. Recovery can handle supported `.aup3` and Audacity unsaved-project files, but cannot recreate samples that no longer exist.

## Steps

1. Open **File > Project management > Recover Audacity project**.
2. Select the damaged project. Read the **Recovery assessment** before importing or saving anything.
3. Inspect **Missing audio ranges**, including additional pages of affected locations.
4. When available, choose **Save repaired Audacity copy**. Otherwise open the recovered result, review its compatibility report, and save your work under a new name.

## Check the result

Play the recovered sections and check gaps or silence against the report. Confirm the new copy opens before relying on it. Retain the original damaged file for another recovery attempt.

## Limits and recovery

Recovery may salvage valid audio prefixes and replace missing samples with silence. A repaired database does not imply that all audio survived or that every native editing feature transfers. Read the reported omissions and never overwrite your only original during diagnosis. For ordinary intact projects, use the [Audacity import guide](/guides/projects/open-an-audacity-project/).
