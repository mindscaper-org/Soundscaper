Use the Analysis panel for live spectrum, loudness, stereo checks, and an ACX audio report.

## Before you start

Open a project with audio. Decide whether you want to inspect playback or a selected range: a time selection can switch the spectrum to selection analysis.

## Steps

1. Open **Analyze > Analysis**. Expand the sections you need, such as **Spectrum**, spectrogram, loudness, or correlation.
2. Clear the time selection and start playback for a live view. Compare track overlays in the spectrum to locate competing frequency bands.
3. Stop and select a passage to inspect its spectrum without playback.
4. For audiobook checks, expand **ACX compliance** and choose **Check audio**. Read the result table; use **Export** for the JSON report.

## Check the result

The live displays respond to playback. An ACX report lists pass or fail for its audio checks rather than merely showing a meter. Correct failing levels or format settings and check again.

## Limits and recovery

A spectrum shows energy, not whether the mix sounds good. Correlation and loudness need context and listening. ACX audio checks do not guarantee acceptance by a publishing service or check every editorial requirement. Collapsing the ACX section clears its displayed check; run it again when needed. Close unneeded analyzer sections to reduce display work.
