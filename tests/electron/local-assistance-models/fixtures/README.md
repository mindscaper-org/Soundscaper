# Real inference fixtures

These small fixtures ship with the nightly test payload. Model weights are downloaded separately through the production installer. `model-inputs.js` verifies the SHA-256 of every committed media file before use.

Speech preparation converts the original 16-bit, 16 kHz mono recording into float32 and adds one second of silence at either end. DeepFilterNet receives a linearly resampled 48 kHz version with reproducible low-level hiss. The astronaut and cat photographs become two 512 × 512 RGBA frames, fitted without distortion on a white canvas and retaining distinct source-frame timing. The astronaut supplies a visible human face and Chelsea supplies a clear object, so the subject test can require results from both networks at the production confidence thresholds. The OCR input is generated locally on a white canvas with black `LOCAL MODEL` and `TEST` text; it contains no third-party media.

Subject correctness requires a face on the astronaut frame (source frame 0) and a COCO cat (class 15) on the Chelsea frame (source frame 1). OCR correctness compares the known rendered text in reading order after Unicode, case, and whitespace normalization. It permits at most a 10% Levenshtein character error rate: at most one edit in this 16-character fixture. Reports retain the expected and recognized text, edit distance, and error rate, including when the case fails. The previously observed `LOCAL MODEI` / `TSET` output has three edits (18.75%) and fails this check.

Speech recognition compares the 22 known JFK words after Unicode, case, and punctuation normalization, joining all transcript segments. The maximum word error rate is 5%, permitting at most one insertion, deletion, or substitution; reports preserve the recognized words and each error count. The synthetic rhythmic fixture has 32 beats at half-second intervals over 16 seconds. Each detected beat or downbeat must lie within 50 ms of its expected position, with a constant tempo proposal within 1 BPM of 120 BPM. The shot fixture has one image switch at source frame 60; exactly one TransNet boundary at frame 59 or 60 is accepted to allow the last-old-frame and first-new-frame conventions. Timing errors, observed tempo, and boundary frames remain in validation reports, including semantic failures.

The tests require real voiced or visual content so that an empty result cannot pass. They check known transcript words, beat timing, shot boundaries, OCR text, and subject identities on their expected frames. They do not compare exact boxes or audio against golden model predictions, and do not establish perceptual restoration or synthesis quality.

Strict real-model cases also require authenticated GPU runtime assets and observed GPU compute in execution profiles or native compute receipts for each required model family. A requested provider alone does not prove execution or identify the physical GPU. The private Windows host sampler correlates inference process IDs with adapter activity when attribution to the RTX 3090 is required.
